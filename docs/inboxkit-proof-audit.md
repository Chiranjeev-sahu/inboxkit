# InboxKit public evidence audit

**Dated source record.** Page observations and inventory counts below retain their original retrieval dates. This file was reviewed for consistency on 18 October 2026, without recrawling the live site. Recheck the relevant source before changing a product claim or preparing a later live campaign.

Checked 15 October 2026. The [sitemap](https://www.inboxkit.com/sitemap.xml) contains 409 URLs. This audit covers **all 56 public partner profiles** and **all 11 case studies** in that sitemap, plus the [homepage](https://www.inboxkit.com/), [pricing](https://www.inboxkit.com/pricing), [agency solution](https://www.inboxkit.com/agency-email-solution), and [Smartlead](https://www.inboxkit.com/learn/inboxkit-smartlead-integration) and [Instantly](https://www.inboxkit.com/learn/inboxkit-instantly-integration) integration guides. It is a complete audit of those decision-relevant sections, not a claim to have read every article on the site.

The 56 partner names, stated websites, domains, profile URLs, and retrieval statuses are in [inboxkit-partner-audit.csv](inboxkit-partner-audit.csv). All 56 profiles resolved a name and domain. The partner and public-customer suppression entries generated from this audit are in [suppression.csv](../data/suppression.csv). Public recognition is enough to keep an account out of a new-customer campaign, but absence from this list does not prove the account is not a customer.
