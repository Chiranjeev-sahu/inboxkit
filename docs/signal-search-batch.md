# Signal discovery batch: 15 October 2026

**Historical experiment, 15 October 2026.** Its counts, fit/signal restrictions and email-policy interpretations describe that test, not the current export policy. Use [decisions](decisions.md), the [current runbook](../skills/lead-research/SKILL.md), and [current campaign outputs](../README.md#review-the-campaign) for present guidance.

This is a search-stage comparison after the first sourcing pilot and the eight-agency strict-signal run. It is a convenience sample, not a market-wide yield estimate. No contact lookup or verification credits were used, and no outreach was sent. Existing pilot agencies are not counted as new leads.

## Rules applied

- Company fit: evidence that the agency manages cold email for clients.
- Hiring candidate: a dated opening tied to client campaign delivery, email operations, or outbound infrastructure, with an identifiable agency.
- Case candidate: a named client and attributable recent work or outcome. A recently edited page alone does not establish recent work or a new client.
- This batch measured discovery only. A full row still needs a named buyer, work email, verification status, and sourced first line.

## What this changes

Search results show a real tradeoff, not a winner. Hiring gives an event with a date and a plausible operational need, but many relevant jobs hide the employer. Case studies readily expose agencies and clients, but a publication or update date often says nothing about a new engagement. One agency can publish many cases and still produces only one preferred buyer row.

First-party pages supplied a named buyer for [Grow Surely](https://growsurely.com/services/cold-email-agency) and [Groundswell](https://groundswellgtm.com/). This supports buyer discoverability for those two accounts, but no work email was found or verified in this batch. The count of additional complete or send-ready rows is therefore zero.

**Do not select the final buying signal yet.** A useful next test is to take a small set of fully identified, source-backed accounts from each group through buyer lookup and independent email verification, then record time and valid-row yield. Treat an active-client-volume signal as a separate hypothesis from a new-client-win signal. If the target is 100 unique agencies, the measured yield must be multiplied by distinct agencies, not case pages or job posts.
