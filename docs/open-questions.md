# Evidence boundaries and validation considerations

This document explains how to interpret the outputs and assess further runs. Operational policies live in the [runbook](../skills/lead-research/SKILL.md); actual row-level uncertainty stays in the contact records and [email review](../deliverables/email-review.md).

| Area | Interpretation | How to evaluate it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prospect relevance | Public outbound activity supports infrastructure relevance, not a commitment to buy. | Retain the fit/signal pages and describe the specific operational fact. |
| Buyer authority | A current founder may plausibly influence spend without being the technical administrator. | Cite the role source and disclose inferred ownership separately from demonstrated responsibility. |
| Email confidence | A provider-positive verdict is probabilistic and can coexist with accept-all=true. | Preserve exact raw assessments, dates and flags; evaluate contradictory clear negatives rather than voting between providers. |
| Alternate domains | A verifier does not prove organizational ownership. | Require public relationship URLs and an explicit ownership judgment before promotion. |
| Public suppression | Public partner/customer pages provide useful exclusions, but private account status requires its own check for an authorized campaign. | Compare company names, company domains and actual email domains, then reconcile private records before activation. |
| Segment capacity | The 100-company sample demonstrates the saved dataset, not the size or yield of every adjacent market. | Track consecutive unique-company dispositions, duplicates and accepted contacts as later batches proceed. |
| Scale and cost | The 2,000-row note separates data-provider allocation from full research/review cost. | Measure actual debits, active time and accepted output; include failed and parked attempts in costs. |
| Conversion assumptions | The strategy memo models contact volume using explicit rates. The draft sample is not a conversion experiment. | An authorized campaign would measure positive replies, bookings, attendance and qualification separately. |
| Runtime access | A clean checkout needs the reader's agent tools and provider authentication. Account balances do not establish endpoint permissions. | Test the specific route, log access failures and use a suitable funded fallback. |
| Verification freshness | Source pages, roles and mailbox routing change. | Preserve observation dates and recheck the specific fact when the next research or activation decision depends on it. |

## Targeted review cases

Chief Lead Officer and Caliber Outbound have provider-positive assessments with explicit exact-address corroboration holds from the targeted review. Leadowski retains conflicting clear verifier results. They remain visible in the research sheet and parked subset, with precise reasons. Promotion depends on resolving each row's issue, not reaching a numeric target.

## Evidence for a new ICP

State the company-fit and operational-signal rules before discovery. Use the supplied segment, inspect first-party evidence, check commercial suitability, select a relevant current buyer and keep each contact's source and verification trail. The saved [SaaS example](../runs/2026-10-18-b2b-saas-outbound-fresh/README.md) shows this process with two draft-ready contacts, two commercial-overlap holds and an invalid address replaced by another buyer.

Provider rankings and discovery strategies are revisable when measured incremental results change. Preserve the reason for a change in that run's notes rather than silently changing eligibility rules.
