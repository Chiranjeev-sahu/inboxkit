GTM Engineer Hiring Assignment
Oct 12, 2026 · @InboxKit
The brief
Design, build and stage a LinkedIn plus email campaign for InboxKit that could book 15 qualified demos in 30 days. Plan for 6 to 8 hours of work, due 5 days after you receive this.
* Product: InboxKit sells cold email infrastructure: mailboxes, domains, DNS setup and warmup that plug into the sequencer a team already uses.
* Buyer: founders and heads of growth at outbound, lead-gen and GTM agencies. The person who owns sending infrastructure, not the AEs who use it.
* Your job: everything from picking the segment to the last follow-up, plus the Claude Code workflow that makes it repeatable.
* Nothing gets sent. Every message stays in draft. We judge the system, not replies.
What to deliver
1. Strategy memo, one page. Which segment and buying signal you chose and why. How LinkedIn and email steps interleave over 3 weeks. The offer and the single CTA. Volume math from 15 demos back to contacts needed, every assumption stated.
2. Lead list, 100 rows in a sheet. Name, title, company, domain, LinkedIn URL, email, verification status, the signal that qualified them, source. Add one paragraph on how this scales to 2,000 rows and the cost per contact.
3. Copy. A 3 to 4 step email sequence with two variants of step 1. A LinkedIn connection note and two follow-ups. Personalised first lines for 10 rows, produced by your workflow, not by hand.
4. Claude Code workflow. A repo with a CLAUDE.md, skills or scripts that turn an ICP description into an enriched, verified, deduped, personalised list ready to import into a sequencer. It must run from a clean checkout. Show how it handles bad emails, duplicates and a suppression list.
Rules
* Every product claim must come from inboxkit.com. Link the page it came from.
* No em dashes or en dashes in any copy. One ask per message. A working opt-out in every email.
* Smartlead and Instantly are integrations, not competitors. You may name them; do not attack anyone by name.
How to submit
Reply to the email that sent you this with one link: a Drive folder or GitHub repo holding the memo, the sheet, the copy, the workflow repo and a Loom of 5 minutes or less.
Shortlisted candidates get a 45 minute review call. You walk us through the work, then we hand you a new segment and you rerun the workflow live.
