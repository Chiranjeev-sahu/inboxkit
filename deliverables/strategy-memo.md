# InboxKit campaign strategy

**18 October 2026 | Draft only | Goal: 15 qualified demos booked in 30 days**

## Segment and buying signal

**Fit:** Founders, heads of growth, and infrastructure owners at agencies delivering client cold email or outbound systems.

**Signal:** Public evidence of client campaign delivery, sending volume, infrastructure setup, expansion, or a relevant hire. One first-party page can establish fit and activity. Record dates and uncertainty; operational relevance does not establish buying intent.

**Why this signal:** Strict hiring searches added five companies from 41 dispositions; strict recent named-client cases added none. We replaced the experimental 90-day cutoff with concrete operational evidence, keeping recency visible.

## Offer and single CTA

Show how InboxKit centralizes client accounts, provides a panel per domain, and automates SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Invite the buyer to **book a [30-minute demo](https://www.inboxkit.com/demo)**. Product evidence: [agency dashboard](https://www.inboxkit.com/agency-email-solution), [domain panels and DNS](https://www.inboxkit.com/pricing). Smartlead and Instantly are integrations. No delivery guarantees or invented savings.

## LinkedIn and email over three weeks

| Week | LinkedIn | Email |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Day 1: connection. Day 4: follow-up 1. | Day 3: email 1A or 1B, with a sourced first line. |
| 2 | Day 10: follow-up 2. | Day 8: email 2. Day 14: email 3. |
| 3 | No further LinkedIn touch. | Day 20: final email. |

LinkedIn follow-ups require an accepted connection; day 4 also requires email 1 to have been sent. Stop both channels on any reply, meeting, or opt-out. Every email includes reply STOP. A monitored reply-to and operator suppression handling are required before live sending. All messages remain drafts.

## Volume math for 15 qualified bookings

**Contacts needed = 15 / qualified-booking rate per unique contact.** Count one eligible agency with a relevant buyer, confirmed infrastructure relevance and a scheduled demo once across both channels. Attendance is a separate downstream measure.

| Planning sensitivity, not observed | Qualified bookings / contact | Contacts for 15 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lower yield | 0.25% | 6,000 |
| Higher yield | 1.00% | 1,500 |

These final qualified-booking rates are explicit assumptions, not observed results or external benchmarks. The examples imply 1,500 to 6,000 unique contacts; campaign volume depends on the rate actually achievable. The 100-row submission demonstrates the workflow and is not claimed to deliver all 15 bookings.

Start with the 97 staged draft-ready contacts; three remain parked. Inspect early delivery and reply handling before expanding. Four steps imply up to four emails per contact, reduced by stop rules. Start final cohorts by day 10 so their day-20 touch fits within day 30. LinkedIn volume depends on account allowance and manual capacity. Confirm sourcing, credits, sending capacity and booking availability before rollout. The [feasibility note](../docs/campaign-feasibility.md) explains the assumptions.
