# Agency scale batch 03

**Date:** 16 October 2026  
**Target:** test whether more dated agency-hiring searches can add qualified companies without weakening the existing acceptance rules.  
**Fit rule:** a public source must show that the company runs managed cold email or outbound campaigns for clients.  
**Signal rule:** a dated hiring page within 90 days must show a role responsible for client campaign operations or sending infrastructure.  
**Contact rule:** an accepted row must identify one relevant buyer and include that person's LinkedIn profile URL.

## Result

- 15 surfaced companies or postings were dispositioned.
- 0 new companies cleared every gate.
- 4 were duplicates of the canonical research or already-open uncertain cases.
- 1 was a publicly suppressed InboxKit partner.
- 3 strong job descriptions were too old for the fixed 90-day test.
- 3 recent postings did not identify the actual hiring company.
- 3 were not usable segment or signal matches.
- 1 remained uncertain because neither an exact date nor a defensible company and buyer chain could be established.
- The canonical totals remain **34 qualified contacts**, including **0 missing LinkedIn profile URLs**.
- No contact-provider credit was spent, no message was sent, and nothing was imported.

## What this batch establishes

The hiring signal still produces high-relevance evidence when it appears, but repeated search queries now return a high share of the same agencies, unnamed employers, and expired listings. The measured conversion across scale batches 01–03 is **5 qualified rows from 41 dispositions**. That result supports expanding the next sourcing batch to the already-approved secondary signal: a dated, named client case with recent campaign activity or results. The fit, suppression, buyer, LinkedIn, and verification gates stay the same.

The zero-add result is evidence about the query and market, not a reason to weaken the sheet. LinkedIn remains mandatory for every accepted buyer; this batch did not create any contact row before that requirement could be met.
