# 20-lead expansion working run

Run date: 15 October 2026. Target: 20 **distinct, complete, qualified** agency buyer rows as a staging milestone toward the brief's 100 rows. Actual result: **20 qualified** with a named buyer, work email, status, signal, sources, and first line; **3 clear-valid/send-ready**. The research-row milestone is met; the send-ready milestone is not. Nothing was sent, enrolled, or imported.

## Rule set before expansion

- ICP: agencies that **run managed cold email for clients**. Company fit and buying signal are separate tests.
- Candidate signal: (1) a relevant agency hiring event or (2) a named client case with attributable cold-email work or results. Date within 90 days is measured as an experiment, not a permanent segment constraint. A recent page edit alone is insufficient.
- One founder or infrastructure/growth owner per company. A named work email must have independent verification. `catch_all` is in the qualified research sheet for review and excluded from send-ready. `invalid` is excluded.
- Exclude known InboxKit customers and partners from new-customer prospecting. No geographic or English-language filter.
- Source-backed first line makes a prospect observation only; no InboxKit product claim is inserted into it.

## Review and bottlenecks

- **ReplyLead:** named case says the engagement is still active, but does not tie its reported volume to a dated result inside the 90-day test. Kept uncertain rather than silently promoting an old account.
- **Scalantec:** first-party team page says active hiring, but the job publication date is unconfirmed. The founder email is clear-valid; the signal is not yet proven recent.
- **Seqnced:** dated named case and founder profile found, but no named work email from Findymail or Lusha. Kept uncertain and out of both exports.
- **VelocityFlow:** Anthony Garcia's LinkedIn profile ties him to the agency and posts about fulfillment. The exact title `Cold email fulfillment lead` is an inference; confirm it before final copy.
- **Shields Outbound:** the job was dated 2 September and sought hires within days. It is a valid dated hiring event, but the vacancy may now be closed. Do not write copy suggesting it is still open.
- **Lead Gen Jay:** also sells mailbox setup; whether InboxKit's offer complements its model needs a positioning check.
- **Six Figure Consulting:** its current site has several unrelated service lines. The job itself confirms management of client outbound campaigns, but this agency should be reviewed before final outreach because outbound may no longer be its main business. The LinkedIn URL was published by Ankit in an older article; direct profile fetch was restricted.
- **ThynkGrowth:** the recent role primarily concerns content and only occasionally cold-email copy. This is a low-strength hiring signal. Keep it as a qualified test row with that caveat, not as proof of an infrastructure purchase.

The practical bottleneck is **clear-valid buyer emails** and signal strength, not a lack of API keys. The 20 qualified rows are a research milestone, not 20 importable contacts. One row uses a weak but relevant hiring signal, and two job boards expose only relative publication dates. The next sourcing experiment should favor stronger dated campaign/deliverability hiring and named client cases, then test a clearly labeled wider date window if the 90-day pool stays thin. Do not reclassify catch-all as valid or uncertain rows as qualified to make a send-ready count. A real CRM/customer suppression list, if the interviewer supplies one, would further change counts.
