# Agency scale batch 04

**Date:** 16 October 2026  
**Target:** test the approved secondary signal: dated, named-client case studies showing recent managed cold-email or outbound campaign activity.  
**Fit rule:** a public source must show that the company runs managed cold email or outbound campaigns for clients.  
**Signal rule:** the evidence must name the client and tie campaign activity or results to the 90-day window beginning 18 July 2026.  
**Contact rule:** only after fit, signal, canonical dedupe, and partner suppression clear may the workflow find one buyer; an accepted buyer must have a LinkedIn profile URL.

## What this batch establishes

Recent case-study search produces real operational evidence, but many agencies anonymize clients. Requiring both a named client and a result inside a rolling 90-day window creates a low-yield signal even when the underlying agency is clearly active. The zero-add result should be kept as evidence rather than converted into weak rows.

The next decision is a signal-policy decision, not a contact-finding problem. A later batch can test one clearly labeled alternative without changing the historical rows: **recent aggregate client campaign scale**, where a dated first-party source reports current multi-client sending or campaign volume. That alternative still requires agency fit, partner suppression, one buyer, a buyer LinkedIn profile, a sourced work email, and visible verification uncertainty.

This batch stops before buyer or email lookup because no company cleared the signal gate. That ordering protects limited credits and keeps LinkedIn complete for all accepted contacts.
