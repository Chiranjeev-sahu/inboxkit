# New ICP live qualification gate

Date: 2026-10-16. Target: at most three qualified, enriched, verified contacts. This is a separate workflow test and does not alter the InboxKit agency campaign.

ICP and evidence rule: [the prior offline smoke test](../2026-10-16-new-icp-offline-smoke/run-notes.md) defined B2B software vendors with first-party proof of an **already operating in-house cold-email outbound motion**, plus a relevant SDR/outbound-operations hiring post published or explicitly reposted from July 18 through October 16, 2026. Employer-authorized first-party evidence must support the product, current sending motion, job duties, publication date, and open status. A job planning to build the motion cannot prove the pre-existing fit criterion. An undated or closed posting does not pass the signal gate. This 90-day threshold is experimental, not a permanent ICP rule.

## What the agent did

Searched public sources for recent cold-email/outbound hiring, opened product and employer job pages, and screened four plausible companies. Candidate dispositions and exact source URLs are in [candidates.csv](candidates.csv). The strongest existing-motion evidence was [Ashby's employer post](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/ashby/6a6184ee-abc9-4a58-8ca3-4221d809a9ab): its scaled outbound lead says the team spent the last year building automated email and LinkedIn campaigns. [Ashby's product page](https://www.ashbyhq.com/) establishes the software. However, the job's application deadline was October 12, and its id was absent from [the employer's currently published jobs feed](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/ashby) when checked October 16. A secondary index suggested an October 6 posting, but that does not make the vacancy current. Ashby remains uncertain, not an accepted lead.

[Cakewalk's software page](https://www.cakewalk.security/) and its [August 10 SDR post](https://talent.seedcamp.com/companies/cakewalk-2/jobs/89354777-sdr) show a product and cold-email hiring, but the post describes an early GTM hire rather than an established motion. [DOSS's platform page](https://www.doss.com/products/platform) and [employer SDR manager post](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/doss/1431038c-9364-4bb9-bccc-a411302879ff) show software and an existing SDR team, but the source does not explicitly prove current cold-email sending or show a publication date. [Probook's product page](https://www.probook.ai/) and [employer SDR post](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/probook/81d50bde-0d98-48f0-a3c1-d8eee6d4a3c0) mention cold email as work for the hire to build, not an existing operation.

No candidate crossed both evidence gates. The agent therefore stopped before person lookup and verification. Icypeas, Datagma, BounceBan, and ZeroBounce were **not called**; no finder/verifier credit was spent. No names, domains, or emails were transmitted to those providers in this run. This is the waterfall's stop condition in action, not a provider failure. No InboxKit product claims or outreach copy were generated. All existing campaign messages remain drafts.

## Measured result

Companies examined: 4. `fit_and_signal`: 0. `fit_only`: 0. `signal_uncertain`: 2 (Ashby has a closed hiring signal; DOSS lacks current-email and date evidence). `not_fit`: 2 under the declared existing-motion rule. Contact lookups: 0. Verifier calls: 0. Qualified rows: 0. Send-ready rows: 0. Duplicates: 0. Suppression matches: 0. First lines: 0. Shortfall against the three-contact test target: 3. The export gate ran successfully on a header-only `leads.csv`; see `audit.json`.

The demonstrated bottleneck is **public evidence for an already-running cold-email motion plus a current dated hiring event**, before email finding. This small search-selected screen does not estimate market-wide lead yield, contact-provider performance, or feasibility of the separate 100-row agency assignment. The next useful experiment is to test a clearly stated, broader new-segment rule, such as an existing in-house SDR/email-sequencing team plus a current relevant hire, then source a small consecutive sample and run the finder/verifier waterfall on accepted accounts. Do not silently count the four candidates above under the original rule.
