## Failures and workflow defects

Official search established https://app.findymail.com/docs/ and correct API host; balance and finder calls worked there. No credit spent on failed host checks. Those errors were agent endpoint selection, not failed provider access. No credential-bearing URL was saved.

Direct LinkedIn profile access returned 999/internal errors. First-party company links/posts and search-index profile content were suitable identity fallbacks; full signed-in profile details were not independently inspected. One provider-found named address was invalid and retained honestly. Old company bylines would have selected a departed CRO without checking the person's own announcement. Commercial-overlap review happened after two enrichments and thus spent avoidable credits. These are visible workflow defects; complete the commercial check before enrichment in future runs. No automated cross-run database exists; this run scanned historical CSVs explicitly. No production yield, delivery outcome or purchase intent is demonstrated by two accepted contacts.
